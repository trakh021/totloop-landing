COMMON QUESTIONS
How does TotLoop verify listings?
Every listing is manually reviewed against the ACCC product safety recall register before it goes live. Sellers declare condition, age, and safety history. Items on the recall register are removed immediately.
Is my payment protected?
Yes. All payments go through secure escrow, held until the buyer confirms the item arrived as described. If there's a dispute, our team steps in. Buyers are never left exposed.
When is TotLoop launching?
We're building our founding member community now. Everyone on the waitlist gets a personal heads-up before we go live — you won't miss it.
What items can I list?
Prams, car seats (under 10 years), cots and nursery furniture, baby carriers, clothing 0–8 years, and toys. Items on the ACCC recall register cannot be listed.
